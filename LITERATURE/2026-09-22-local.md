## Part B — Implications for SESPy (`sespy/network.py`, `dynamics`, wizard)

**1. Quillet et al. — VALIDATE + EXTEND.** Their SEN is typed by layer (environmental / economic / productive / social) and the headline finding is structural: which category sits at the core. SESPy already has `subsystem()`, `social_ecological_fit()` and `hypermodules()`, but no explicit **core–periphery / layer-mixing** read-out. A `core_periphery(isa)` built on `nx.core_number` (k-core) plus an intra- vs inter-group edge-density block matrix over DAPSI(W)R(M) types would reproduce their descriptive result on any imported ISA and give a defensible "which element type holds the system together" sentence for the report. At 227 nodes their graph is also a realistic stress test for the ~12-node Boolean cap and the simplification routines. *Tension:* none — purely descriptive, fits the "simple SES" goal.

**2. Montanari et al. — CHALLENGE.** SES adjacency matrices are signed and asymmetric (nonreciprocal), i.e. non-normal. The Science paper shows stability intuitions derived from symmetric/Hermitian reductions do not transfer. SESPy's Laplacian/eigenvalue-spectrum module reports spectral stability only; for strongly non-normal matrices the spectrum can be misleading about transient amplification. Cheap fix: report **Henrici's departure from normality** (‖A‖_F² − Σ|λ_i|²)^½ and the **reciprocity** of the signed digraph next to the spectrum, with a one-line caveat when non-normality is high. *Tension:* adds a diagnostic that stakeholders will not read — keep it in the technical appendix of the export only.

**3. Familkhalili et al. — EXTEND.** SESPy's Monte Carlo already perturbs connections by confidence (`_perturbed_connections`, `uncertainty_scores`) and Behaviour-Over-Time plots trajectories. What is missing is the **categorical synthesis**: per node, the share of ensemble runs in which the endpoint state crosses a threshold → label *robust-up / robust-down / uncertain*. This is exactly the "fate map" step and is what a manager reads. Quick to add on top of existing ensemble output.

**4. Katende — VALIDATE + EXTEND.** Structural ablation ≈ `intervention_impact`/`remove_nodes` (validated). What SESPy lacks is **global sensitivity**: Latin-hypercube sampling over the MC hyper-parameters (perturbation base rate, flip mode, damping/step) with **partial rank correlation coefficients** against summary outputs. Complements the 2026-08-25 M1 topology-sensitivity item. *Tension:* moderate complexity; ship as a CLI/notebook helper, not a wizard step.

**5. Indradewa et al. — no action.** Confirms the CLD→scenario→leverage narrative; SESPy's linear iteration is the deliberately lightweight stand-in for a full stock-flow model.

**6. Ocan et al. — VALIDATE.** AHP-derived edge weights + scenario Δ ranking is structurally what `bayesian_consensus` + `intervention_impact` already do. Note AHP pairwise comparison as an optional weighting mode if raters ask for it; not worth an issue now.
