## Implications for SESPy

**MPA fisheries governance power dynamics (key paper).** Comparative social-ecological network analysis of fisheries/MPA governance at two Senegalese sites, unpacking power asymmetries between actor types (fishers, local authorities, co-management bodies). This is directly in the MarineSABRES application domain. It goes one step beyond the governance gap detection in open issue #13 (binary: governance actor linked or unlinked to an ecological node) toward *how much* influence each governance actor exerts. SESPy's existing centrality trio (betweenness, eigenvector, PageRank) applies uniformly across all node types; a governance-actor-specific centrality slice — restricting the computation to R/M-typed nodes and expressing their relative influence rank within the social-ecological graph — is a natural, low-risk extension that would immediately aid MarineSABRES practitioners diagnosing co-management imbalances. Distinct enough from #13 to warrant a separate issue. Caveat: full paper not accessible via proxy; specific computational approach inferred from title and journal scope only.

**Encounters of the Social in SES Resilience (Annual Review).** Conceptual review of how social agency, power, and equity are theorised within SES resilience frameworks. Validates SESPy's DAPSIWR(M) node-type classification (which explicitly distinguishes social response/management nodes from ecological state/impact/pressure nodes) as a recognised structured approach to capturing social actors in SES models. Purely conceptual; no algorithmic extension.

**FCM applications in behavioural science.** Survey of fuzzy cognitive mapping use across behavioural-science domains. Confirms FCM is actively applied well beyond its natural-science origins. FCM edge-weight import was shipped as #2 (closed); this paper does not suggest a novel SESPy feature not already covered by the existing implementation.

**Beyond silos — energy interdependencies.** A conceptual framework for cross-sector interdependency mapping in energy transitions. The systems perspective overlaps philosophically with CLDs, but the energy-transition domain, the multi-sector scope, and the absence of any graph-based formalism place it outside SESPy's marine SES focus. Not actionable.

**Grey causal modelling in public service.** Applies Grey Relational Analysis (GRA) — a data-driven technique from Grey System Theory — to identify causal relationships in public-service challenges. "Grey causal" is distinct from CLD-based loop analysis; GRA requires time-series data SESPy does not hold. Non-actionable within the tool's qualitative scope.
